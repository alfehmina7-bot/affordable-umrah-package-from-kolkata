# Umrah Package from Kolkata

A practical travel guide for pilgrims planning an Umrah journey from Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

## About This Guide

Planning Umrah involves more than simply comparing package prices. Travellers should consider flights, visa assistance, accommodation in Makkah and Madinah, transportation, room arrangements, trip duration and the level of support included in the package.

This repository brings together useful information to help Indian pilgrims understand the main points to check before selecting an Umrah package from Kolkata.

## What to Check Before Booking

### 1. Flight Arrangements

Check the departure and return flights, baggage allowance, travel dates and whether the flight itinerary suits your overall pilgrimage schedule.

### 2. Umrah Visa Assistance

Ask what visa-related assistance is included and what documents are required before travelling.

### 3. Hotel Location

Hotel distance and location can make a significant difference during an Umrah journey. Check the accommodation details for both Makkah and Madinah before booking.

### 4. Room Sharing

Different packages may offer different room-sharing arrangements. Confirm whether the quoted price is based on double, triple or quad sharing.

### 5. Transportation

Ask whether airport transfers and transportation between Makkah and Madinah are included in the package.

### 6. Trip Duration

Compare the total number of days as well as how many nights are planned in Makkah and Madinah.

### 7. Additional Costs

Before confirming a package, ask about costs that may not be included in the initial quotation, such as optional services, additional transportation or changes to travel arrangements.
